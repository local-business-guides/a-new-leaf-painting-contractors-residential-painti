What a Jacksonville painting contractor covers

A project may start with a review of the surfaces and a written estimate. Depending on the property, the work may include washing, drywall or stucco repairs, carpentry or wood rot repairs, and then painting. Cabinets and garage floors call for their own coating work, while commercial projects may have different spaces and work needs. The estimate should make clear which of these tasks are part of your project.

A few terms you may hear:
- Preparation: Steps taken before coating, which can include cleaning and surface repairs.
- Coating: The paint or other finish applied to a surface.
- Scope: The specific work included in the estimate.

Ask each provider to spell out the scope and what could change it if additional surface issues are found. A “Contractor Jacksonville” search is a starting point, but a clear conversation about your property is more useful than a broad label.
