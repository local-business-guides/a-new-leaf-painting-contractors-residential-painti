About A New Leaf Painting Contractors, Jacksonville.

A New Leaf Painting ContractorsUpdated 2026-11-25

A New Leaf Painting Contractors is an insured painting company based in Jacksonville, Florida, serving Northeast Florida since 2010. The owner brings experience dating to 1988. Our work includes interior and exterior painting, cabinet and commercial painting, drywall and stucco repair, wood rot and carpentry repairs, pressure washing, and garage floor coatings.

We use Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore coatings. We focus on thorough preparation, protecting the work area, and keeping job sites clean. You can ask us about the scope of your project and receive a clear written estimate. Our work is backed by a written warranty, and the company has more than 700 five-star reviews across Google and other platforms.

To discuss a project or schedule a free estimate, call +1904-615-6599. Our office is at 11246 Distribution Ave E Ste 16, Jacksonville, FL 32256. We are open daily, Monday through Sunday, from 7:00 to 19:00.
