From surface review to finished coating

Painting work can begin with a discussion of the areas you want done and a review of their condition. Depending on the surface, the project may call for pressure washing, drywall or stucco repair, or wood rot and carpentry repairs before painting. Interior, exterior, cabinet, commercial, and garage floor work each has its own requirements, so the estimate should name the specific areas and services.

These terms may come up while you plan:
- Surface preparation: Cleaning and other work done to ready a surface for coating.
- Coating: Paint or another finish applied to the surface.
- Written scope: The listed tasks and areas included in the estimate.

Ask how the scope addresses repairs and preparation, and how any added work would be discussed. These details help you understand what you’re agreeing to before the job begins.
